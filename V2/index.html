"Welcome to SalesPulse! I'm your AI presenter and I'm here to walk you through this analytics platform. SalesPulse was built as a university project to demonstrate how modern web technologies turn raw transaction data into actionable business insights. The platform reads 3,636 real coffee purchases from index_1.csv — dates, payment methods, card numbers and drink names — and converts every USD amount into tenge using a live exchange rate.
