Analysis & Insights

Overview

  • Dataset: covers 3,636 transactions from 1 March 2024 to 23 March 2025.
  • Total revenue: $115,431.58 (average check: $31.75).
  • Live currency: prices convert to KZT using the current USD/KZT rate shown in the banner.
  • Scope: focuses on transaction volume, revenue and payment methods — there are no city, cost or profit fields in the source data.

Key Findings

  • Top revenue: Latte leads with $27,866.30 (782 cups).
  • Most popular: Americano with Milk leads by volume with 824 cups ($25,269.12).
  • Peak months: October 2024 was highest at $13,891.16 (426 transactions), followed closely by February 2025.
  • Category split: Coffee drives ~84% of revenue; hot chocolate and cocoa make up the remaining ~16%.

Customer Behaviour

  • Card dominance: 3,547 of 3,636 transactions (~98%, $112,245.58) were paid by card; only 89 used cash.
  • Seasonality: activity peaks in October and February and drops to its lowest in January and April.

Conclusions

  • Menu focus: revenue is concentrated in 8 drinks, led by Latte, Americano with Milk and Cappuccino.
  • Card-first promos: since ~98% of orders are by card, digital receipts and card-linked loyalty perks will reach almost everyone.
  • Staffing: scale staff up for October and February peaks and trim shifts in January and April.
